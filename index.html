Overview

LOOT, PROGRESS, CONQUER!

Vault Hunters is an Action RPG Roguelike overhaul of Minecraft. Explore procedurally generated Vaults, collect powerful loot, and unlock new abilities. Take on the challenge solo or team up with friends to conquer together. Your skills will be tested, your strategy questioned, and your courage challenged. Do you have what it takes to become a Vault Hunter?

moss

MEET YOUR ENEMIES

Encounter dangerous mobs with unique abilities and attack patterns as you venture deeper into the vault depths. Each enemy presents distinct challenges.

skills

explore 150+ skills

Master diverse abilities, spells, and perks to customize your playstyle and conquer the vault.

abilities talents expertise prestige powers

Abilities typically focus on giving you powerful active skills that significantly change how you play the game; ranging from damaging abilities like Nova or Rampage, healing abilities like Heal and Totem, and survivability abilities such as Dash and Ghost Walk. Every ability can also be specialised - either subtly changing their effect, or completely revamping it. As you progress through Vault Hunters you get to select the abilities that most suit your playstyle!
